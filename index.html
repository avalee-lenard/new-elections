A few helpful starting points for understanding the process and finding your place in it.

Know what’s on the ballot

Learn which offices and questions are up for a vote. Nonpartisan voter guides can help you compare candidates and understand local measures.

Your next steps

A simple plan goes a long way.

Take a few minutes to get familiar with the process. Use official sources for the rules and dates where you live.

  • 1
    Find your official election office

    Look up your state or local election website for accurate, location-specific information.

  • 2
    Review registration and deadlines

    Check your registration status and confirm the dates and requirements that apply to you.

  • 3
    Choose how you’ll participate

    Explore the voting options available in your area and decide what fits your schedule.

  • 4
    Get informed and make a plan

    Read reliable sources about the candidates and issues, then put your plan on the calendar.

Keep it local. Election dates, registration rules, and voting methods vary by location. Always verify current details with your official election authority.
